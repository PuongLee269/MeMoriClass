Lượt này thiết kế lại nhân vật Bọ: bọ rùa, dễ thương, 5 phương án, có phương án cá tính, phương án ngốc.

Edit the first image only. ## 2. Năm phương án nhân vật Bọ (bo-1 … bo-5)
Ảnh đưa vào: `phuong-an/pa-13.png` và `phuong-an/pa-15.png` (chỉ lấy phong cách nét manga).

### PHẦN CHUNG NHÂN VẬT
```
Character design sheet for "Bọ", the mascot of a Vietnamese learning website: a small LADYBUG, cute and appealing. Match the black-and-white Japanese manga ink style of the reference images (loose confident G-pen lines, natural line-weight variation, a little screentone for shading), on clean neutral white paper. No colour, no yellow paper.
Must read instantly as a ladybug: round dome shell split down the middle, clear round spots on the shell, small head, two antennae with ball tips, six short legs. Keep the silhouette simple and round so it still reads at 48 px.
Layout of the sheet (one image, 16:9): left, a large full-body view in 3/4 front; middle, a front view and a side view; right, a column of 4 small head expressions: happy, surprised, thinking, sleepy. Under the large view, a small sketch of Bọ sitting on a mossy log as on the website. No text labels, no names, no colour swatches.
Design for animation: clear separate shapes for shell, head, eyes, antennae and legs.
```

### Bọ 1: Dễ thương ngây thơ (chuẩn linh vật)
```
Personality: sweet and innocent. Very round body, huge glossy manga eyes with two white highlights, tiny smile, soft blush hatching on cheeks, antennae curved like question marks. Gentle, safe, friendly mascot that children and parents both love.
```

### Bọ 2: Cá tính, tự tin
```
Personality: cool and confident, a little sassy. Half-lidded eyes with a sharp confident look, a small smirk, one antenna straight and one bent at an angle, a tiny leaf worn like a scarf or bandana. Slightly flatter, more stylish shell shape with bold black spots, stance with one leg on hip. Still cute, never mean.
```

### Bọ 3: Ngốc nghếch, ngộ nghĩnh
```
Personality: goofy and silly. Mismatched eyes (one bigger than the other, looking slightly different directions), tongue poking out, antennae tangled or flopping, shell a bit lopsided, one spot drawn as a smudge, clumsy pose about to trip. Funny and lovable, the kind of character that makes people laugh.
```

### Bọ 4: Ham học, tò mò
```
Personality: curious little scholar, fits a learning class. Tiny round glasses, big curious eyes, antennae perked up high, holding a small leaf like a notebook or a tiny pencil. Shell spots arranged neatly. Thoughtful, smart and warm.
```

### Bọ 5: Tinh nghịch, năng động
```
Personality: energetic and mischievous. Shell slightly open showing tiny wings mid-flutter, wide grin showing one tooth, sparkling eyes, antennae bouncing with small motion lines, dynamic jumping pose. Full of energy, playful.
```

## Tiêu chí chấm
- Nhận ra bọ rùa ngay; dễ thương; đọc được ở cỡ nhỏ 48 px.
- Cá tính rõ khác nhau giữa 5 phương án.
- Cùng nét manga với PA13/PA15; ghép vào trang không lạc.
- Hình khối đơn giản, tách phần rõ để làm chuyển động.
