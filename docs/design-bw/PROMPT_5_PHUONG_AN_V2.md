# Vòng 2: 5 phương án đen trắng mới cho trang Bọ (pa-6 … pa-10)

Phản hồi chủ dự án sau vòng 1: PA3 (Sách tranh) gần nhất nhưng chưa đúng. Giấy phải trắng hoặc xám trung tính, KHÔNG ngả vàng/kem/sepia. Nghiên cứu thêm hội họa đen trắng, comic, manga; hiện đại một chút nhưng không công nghiệp (không vector công ty, không 3D bóng bẩy, không gradient số).

Rút ra từ video tham khảo "Explore the Space":
- Nền: trắng lạnh (#F4F4F4) và xám trung tính phẳng (#D6D6D6), không vân giấy vàng.
- Mảng mực: đen tuyệt đối, PHẲNG, mép bo hữu cơ, không nét khắc bên trong (chỉ vài chấm sao trắng nhỏ).
- Chi tiết khắc chỉ nằm trên vật thể RỜI (phi hành gia, hành tinh, kính thiên văn), như hình cắt dán trôi trên nền. Không vẽ phong cảnh kín toàn màn hình.
- Khoảng trống rất lớn; 3 sắc độ chính: trắng, xám, đen.
- Chữ tiêu đề khối retro dày, bóng đổ đen lệch.

## PHẦN CHUNG V2 (dán cho cả 5 ảnh)
```
Redesign this website screenshot as a finished black-and-white web page design. Keep the SAME layout and the same UI elements in the same places: logo "Me.Mori CLASS" top-left, small map icon top-right, the speech bubble near the top-centre containing the label "Bọ", the text "Chào bạn, chào mừng bạn đến với Mori Class. Bạn có muốn tham quan một vòng không?", a small map thumbnail and the link "Tham quan bản đồ"; directly below it a thin input line "Hỏi Me.Mori…" with a paper-plane icon; the cute round ladybug mascot "Bọ" (big round eyes, two antennae with ball tips) sitting on a small mossy log at the right; two small round buttons bottom-right. Copy all Vietnamese text exactly, with correct diacritics. No other text.
Colour: strict neutral monochrome. Background is clean cool white (#F4F4F4) and/or flat neutral grey (#D6D6D6). Absolutely NO yellow, cream, beige, sepia or warm paper tint. Pure black #0A0A0A for ink.
Composition (like the second reference image, a modern black-and-white landing page): do NOT draw a full landscape. Instead, a few ISOLATED hand-drawn objects float on a calm flat background — the moon, one tree branch with leaves, a few river stones, the log with Bọ, two or three leaves or fireflies. Large areas of empty flat background. Organic black ink blobs are perfectly flat solid black with soft rounded liquid edges (no hatching inside them, at most a few tiny white star dots).
Tonal control: only 3 main values (white, one flat grey, black). Fine detail only on the objects, never on the background. Highest contrast only at the speech bubble and Bọ. Eye order: speech bubble → Bọ → input line → logo/icons. Generous space around the text, readable on a phone.
Feel: contemporary editorial / art-book design, hand-made marks, crafted and warm in spirit but neutral in colour. Not corporate, not flat vector clip-art, no glossy 3D, no digital gradients, no drop-shadow UI cards.
Flat 16:9 web screenshot, crisp, high detail, no device mockup, no frame.
```

## Phương án 6: Mảng mực phẳng + vật thể khắc rời (sát video nhất)
```
Direction: "Flat ink blobs, etched cut-outs". Exactly like the reference landing page: cool white upper area, a large flat grey band, and 3 big flat black blobs (one behind the moon top-right, one behind Bọ and the log, one small at the bottom-left). Each object is a detailed pen-and-ink etching (hatching, stippling) cut out and floating, slightly overlapping the blob edges. Bọ is drawn white-on-black inside the blob with black spots outlined in white.
```

## Phương án 7: Manga tram chấm (screentone)
```
Direction: "Manga screentone". Clean confident manga ink lines with varied brush weight; greys made ONLY with mechanical halftone dot screentone (visible dots), not hatching. White page, one soft screentone dot area behind the scene, one flat black blob behind Bọ. Bọ drawn as a cute manga mascot: crisp black outline, solid black shell with white spots, large sparkling eyes with white highlights. The speech bubble is a classic manga speech balloon (white, bold smooth black outline, small tail pointing to Bọ). A few subtle manga speed/sparkle lines around Bọ only.
```

## Phương án 8: Khắc lino hiện đại
```
Direction: "Modern linocut print". Bold, simplified shapes carved from black: flat solid black areas with white carved gouge lines that follow the form (moon, branch, stones, log). Few, large, graphic shapes; Scandinavian-modern print feel, slight hand-printed edge roughness, no texture noise in the background. Background clean white with one flat grey rectangle-free organic area. Bọ is a solid black linocut silhouette with carved white spots and white eyes.
```

## Phương án 9: Mực tàu (sumi-e) + giao diện sạch
```
Direction: "Sumi-e ink wash". The ink blobs are expressive single brush strokes and ink pools of black sumi ink with dry-brush edges on clean white paper; the moon is a pale empty circle left in a grey ink wash; the branch is a few calligraphic strokes. Only 3 values: white, one light grey wash, black. Bọ is drawn with crisp fine ink line and solid black shell, cute and clear. The UI (speech bubble, input line) stays crisp, minimal and modern against the painterly marks.
```

## Phương án 10: Comic nét đều (ligne claire)
```
Direction: "Ligne claire comic". European clear-line comic style: every object drawn with one uniform clean black line weight, no hatching, filled with flat areas of white, one light grey and solid black only. Isolated objects (moon, branch, stones, log, Bọ) on a white page with one large flat black blob behind Bọ. Bọ: round, friendly, solid black shell with white spots, big white eyes. Speech bubble as a clean comic balloon with uniform black outline.
```

## Tiêu chí chấm
- Nền trắng/xám trung tính, không vàng.
- Nhìn 2 giây biết đọc khung thoại → Bọ → ô hỏi.
- Chỉ 3 sắc độ lớn; nền phẳng, chi tiết chỉ ở vật thể.
- Chữ khung thoại đọc rõ cỡ điện thoại; Bọ dễ thương, nhận ra ngay.
